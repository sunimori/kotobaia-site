Website privacy

Updated September 11, 2026

Visiting this website

This website introduces Sunimori and Kotobaia. It does not require an account and does not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.

Language preference

If you change the display language, your preference is saved in your browser’s local storage. It is not sent to Sunimori as a profile or tracking identifier. You can remove it by clearing this website’s browser storage.

Email and external links

Contact links open your own email application. If you send us an email, we use the information you provide to respond to your request. External websites, including app stores and service providers, have their own privacy policies.

For how the Kotobaia app handles data, read the Kotobaia privacy policy.
